Legacy Night — Quick Guide

What each part does, and where to find it

Three ways to check a guest in. All three do exactly the same thing behind the scenes, so you can mix them freely.
📱
Doors Mode — phone camera

Pick your lane (1, 2 or 3), then scan. The guest's table and seat appear immediately. Your phone won't sleep while this is open.

⌨️
Hardware scanner

Plug a USB or Bluetooth scanner into the desk PC — no setup needed. It works alongside the camera, so each desk has two independent ways to scan. A "⌨ Scanner" badge confirms each read.

✅
Check-In tab — by name

For anyone without a working QR code. Search their name, confirm, done. Companions are searchable too, so the +1 can arrive first.

A guest can also check themselves in at the Guest Kiosk screen — useful when no one is free.
🆔
Track ID

Every guest has a short reference like LN-4KX7, sent with their ticket. If a name is hard to catch, ask for this instead — it works in the Check-In search and on the kiosk.

⏰
Overdue

Who replied but hasn't arrived. Mark a no-show here once you're confident.

📊
Overview

Live headcount, arrivals, VIPs, seating progress and catering numbers per person.

📋
Guest List

Every booking, with companions shown on their own indented row. Click any row to open it. Search covers companions and organisations.

🎫
Approvals

Sends each guest their ticket — one email per person, each with their own QR code. Use Send All to work through everyone waiting; it sends one at a time so a single failure can't stop the rest, and re-running only retries what failed.

📱
QR Generator

For guests with no email — builds a ticket image to send by WhatsApp.

🪑
Tables

Two views. List shows every person with their seat. Floor plan is a drag-and-drop map of the ballroom — drag a guest onto a table or a specific chair, or switch to Move mode to rearrange tables and add cocktail stands. Save the layout so every screen matches.

🗂
Categories

Drag every invited guest into their group — PBIS Parents, Diplomatic Guests and so on. A ✓ marks those who have replied, so you can see which groups are actually turning up.

🛡
Verify

Checks every RSVP against the official invitation list. Flags likely typos and anyone who replied without being invited.

⭐
VIP

Starred guests with arrival status, so you know who still needs greeting.

Online RSVP is closed. New guests are added from the Guest List tab using + Add Guest. Anyone who tries to RSVP on the public page is asked to contact us instead.
Open these from the top bar. Log in on the device once beforehand — the session lasts 14 days.
📺
Open Monitor

The welcome screen behind the desk. Choose a lane so it shows only that desk's arrivals. Each name stays up to 20 seconds; Continue skips to the next, and a new arrival takes over immediately.

🙋
Guest Kiosk

Self-service. A guest scans their ticket to check themselves in, or types their name or Track ID to find their seat. Shows name, table and seat only.

🗺
Guest Screen

A touchscreen seating chart for guests — tap a table to see who's there, or search a name to have their table light up. Opened from the Tables tab.

🖨
Print Sheet

Paper backup for the desk: every guest by table and A–Z, with seats, meals and tick boxes. Print this before the event.

📷
Camera won't open

Allow camera access when asked. If you dismissed it, close the tab and reopen. Use name search meanwhile — it checks people in the same way.

🔍
A QR code won't scan

Ask the guest to raise their screen brightness and hold the phone at arm's length rather than close. If it still won't read, search their name.

📶
"Can't reach the server"

Keep checking people in. Arrivals are saved on the device and sync automatically once the connection returns — a "⏳ pending" badge shows how many are waiting. Nothing is lost.

🙋
Someone isn't on the list

Search their companion's name and their organisation too — they may be a guest of another booking. If they genuinely aren't there, take their details on paper.

↩️
"Already checked in"

Another lane got there first, or the ticket was scanned twice. Their table and seat still show — read those out and let them through.

🔒
Approve button missing

That guest has no email address. Use the QR Generator tab and send by WhatsApp.
